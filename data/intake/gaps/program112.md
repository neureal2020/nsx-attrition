# Program 112: University of Vermont Medical Center Program (Burlington, VT), Phase 1 + Phase 2 gap report

This is a 7-year program with a complement of 1 per year: "We have a small program with one resident per year" (landing page, Common Crawl CC-MAIN-2018-39) and "graduates one chief resident a year" (med.uvm.edu/surgery/education/residency, Wayback 2017-09). PGY-5 is spent at Maine Medical Center, and later at Utah. There is no `programs.website`. The roster has always lived on the sponsoring hospital's GME site, not on the UVM Division of Neurosurgery pages.

## Phase 2 searches
- **Common Crawl retries:** CC-MAIN-2018-39 has only the landing page (no names, but it states "one resident per year"). CC-MAIN-2017-04 and CC-MAIN-2019-51 have 0 records. I also scanned every 2016-2020 crawl for the UVM Surgery department hosts (uvm.edu/medicine/surgery, uvm.edu/larnermed/surgery, med.uvm.edu/surgery). They hold only the faculty-only neurosurgery division page.
- **Wayback:** I ran gapaudit for 2016-2020 on uvmhealth.org/gme, gme.uvmhealth.org, med.uvm.edu/surgery, uvm.edu/medicine/surgery, uvm.edu/larnermed/surgery and med.uvm.edu/neurosurgery. I also ran domain-wide CDX queries for any "neurosurg" URL on uvmhealth.org and fletcherallen.org. There is no residents page before 2020-08. About 120 archived med.uvm.edu/surgery pages from 2016-19 (directory, residency, news, bios) name no neurosurgery residents.
- **Cross-program:** Utah (program 111) lists UVM's PGY-5s as visitors: Ducis 2015-16, Akture 2016-17, none in 2017-18, 2018-19 unobserved, Limoges 2019-20, Muse 2020-21. Brown's (program 54) 2018-19 roster lists "Xun Li, M.D., Temple University School of Medicine", PGY-5, which is the same person.
- **PubMed/Europe PMC affiliation mining** (Vermont AND neurosurgery, 2013-2022) found no unknown residents. The Vermont-affiliated names that are not residents (Gelinne, Donaldson, Leung) are UVM medical students. OpenAlex returned 429.
