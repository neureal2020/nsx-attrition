Rosters were corrected (2013-14 Gamble PGY3; 2014-15 Beecher/Loven PGY7, Januszewski/Virojanapa PGY6).

- **Unadkat** did a BWH general-surgery year in 2018-19, then joined the 2018 class at PGY2 in July 2019. He was off the roster in 2021-22 for a research year (Feinstein/Elmezzi), which moved him from Class of 2025 to Class of 2026. His 2026 graduation is inferred.
- **Kevin Shah**'s 2023 graduation is inferred: he is absent from the observed 2023-24 roster, his papers carry a Northwell neurosurgery affiliation through Sep 2025, and his NPI shows a neurosurgery practice in NC.
