# Program 97: University of New Mexico School of Medicine (Albuquerque NM)

Program length 7 (new program, 1 per year). The old program trained pre-2013 entrants for 6 years and 2013+ entrants for 7 (`terminal_pgy_by_entry_year` in the JSON). Department founded 2006.

## Two programs in sequence (phase 2: closure documented)
- **Old program:** ACGME withdrew accreditation **effective 2020-06-30**. An ACGME Office of Complaints letter (Aug 2018) listed 7 allegations, including a falling caseload, residents pulled from the OR for service tasks, retaliation and no pediatric/spine/functional faculty. The program was put on probation, had a site visit in summer 2019, and was told of the withdrawal in Aug 2019. There were **10 residents in 2019-20**: 2 finished in June 2020 and 8 (with 1-6 years left) had to transfer, with UNM paying their salaries. Sources: Albuquerque Journal 2019-10-17 (abqjournal.com/1379907, via Common Crawl 2019-47), Daily Lobo 2019-10-14 (Wayback), UNM HSC news 2021-10-26 (Wayback). The Daily Lobo's "8 residents, 6 already gone" conflicts with the Journal. Destination rosters show the moves took effect in July 2020.
- **No residents** in 2020-21 or 2021-22 (not_applicable).
- **New program** accredited 2022-04-01. First resident Kazim started 2022-07-01.
