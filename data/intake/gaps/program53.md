# Program 53: Penn State Milton S. Hershey Medical Center (neurosurgery)

7-year program. Class size: 2 per year through the 2018 entrants and 3 per year from 2019. The program page says "accepting two residents per year" in 2012 and "admits two residents per year" from Oct 2017 to Jul 2019, then "admits three residents per year" from Sep 2019. The program went from 6 to 7 years with the 2005 entrants: Douds (2004 entrant) finished at PGY-6 in 2010, and Horton and Rizk (2005 entrants) finished at PGY-7 in 2012. Koijan Kainth, who transferred in, is an individual exception (he graduated after PGY-6 in 2013).

**Why the 2017-21 and 2022-24 gaps are now rated low.**
- Every resident on the 2016-17 roster is accounted for. The alumni list shows each graduating in the expected year, and Jafrani joined in July 2017.
- The 2020 commencement page names a single "2020 graduate". That is Davanzo; his classmate Ziu had left in 2016.
- Each entry class from 2017 to 2023 is at its stated complement (2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3) on the Feb 2022 or Sep 2024 roster. The exception is the 2021 class, where the known loss is Madden. No class is above its complement.
- The alumni list (graduates 2012-2024) names no one missing from the rosters, so no unseen resident graduated.
- PubMed affiliation mining for 2016-2025 found no unlisted resident.

The remaining unseen scenario is narrow: an entrant who left and was replaced by a transfer into the same class, where both were never recorded. Partial evidence rules this out for Fritch (July 2020 nomination as an intern), Tuohy (Research Day 2023 as an intern), Kray, and the other residents placed above.
