# Program 66: Tulane University / Ochsner Clinic Foundation (New Orleans, LA), Phase 1 gap report

This was a 6-year program through the 2012 entrants (Kahn graduated in 2018 after PGY-6) and has been 7 years since the 2013 entrants (Umansky graduated in 2020). The program history article (Neurosurgery 2019, linked from the live site) says "We alternate between taking 1 and 2 residents a year", so I recorded the complement as about 2.

**History:** the program is sponsored by Tulane. It was renamed Tulane University/Ochsner Clinic Foundation in 2009, after LSU withdrew its residency from Ochsner in 2008 (Ochsner had been an LSU site from 1993 to 2008). Ochsner had no separate neurosurgery residency in the study window, and it hosts no roster pages: I checked ochsner.org, education.ochsner.org and academics.ochsner.org by Wayback prefix. `first_class_year` is null.
