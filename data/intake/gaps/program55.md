# Program 55: Riverside University Health System Program (Moreno Valley, CA), formerly the Arrowhead Regional Medical Center (ARMC) AOA neurosurgery residency. Gap report (phase 1 + phase 2, 2026-09-28)

## History
- Founded in 2000 by Javed Siddiqi at Arrowhead Regional Medical Center (Colton, CA) as an AOA (osteopathic) program (ARMC/WesternU/OPTI-West). The first graduate was Darryl Warner in 2003. The program called itself "the largest AOA-accredited neurosurgery residency in the country".
- The RUHS page (Sep 2016) says: "Effective July 1, 2016, Riverside University Health System will be the sponsoring institution for this program". The ACGME program 1600500129 is the same lineage. The PD is Dan Miulli (previously associate PD), and ruhealth.org describes it as "Initial ACGME accreditation … 7-year track".
- In the AOA era, residents finished after **PGY-6**, with PGY-1 as the osteopathic internship year (e.g. Mahato: PGY-2 2011-12 → PGY-6 2015-16 → alumnus 2016). The ACGME program is 7 years. `program_length` is set to 7, but every class observed here graduated after PGY-6.
- **Phase-2 correction:** ARMC's "Neurosurgery (AOA) Residency Program" page (2025-26; PD Javed Siddiqi, coordinator Mariel Gray at ARMC) is **not** this program. The RUHS program's PD is Miulli and its coordinator is Jose Gonzalez at RUHS. The ARMC page is the AOA teach-out of the former **Desert Regional Medical Center (Palm Springs)** AOA program. Its residents (Houston, Arshad, Ramnot, Reier, Whitney) publish as DRMC / "Arrowhead Neurosurgical Medical Group, Palm Springs" from 2020, and PMID 37033513 treats RUHS and DRMC as two separate programs. Rebecca Houston (ARMC news 2024-03, PGY5) belongs to that lineage.
- The complement is about 3 per year. There were 21 residents over 6 PGYs in 2016-17. A 2026 RUHS paper (PMID 42255808) tabulates **18 residents** in one recent year. RUHS contributed 33 residents (5 women) to a 2015-21 case-log study (PMID 37033513).
- **Terminal PGY by cohort:** 6 for entries up to 2013 (AOA), and 7 from the 2014 cohort (ACGME). Evidence: a PGY-7 in a 2020 simulator study (PMID 33425512), Krel and Majeed starting fellowships in mid-2021, and Bowen as PGY-7 in 2022-23 (PMID 36601142). This is recorded as `terminal_pgy_by_entry_year`.

## Searches (phase 2)
- **PubMed:** every author whose own affiliation names RUHS, ARMC, RCRMC, Moreno Valley or Colton together with neurosurgery, 2008-26 (1942 PMIDs). Also Desert Regional / Palm Springs neurosurgery authors, and per-candidate histories.
- **OpenAlex:** RUHS (I2801969400) and ARMC (I2799587377) works 2015-26, plus author affiliation histories.
- **Europe PMC:** 195 open-access full texts searched for PGY and resident statements.
- **NPPES:** all trainees and neurosurgeons in Moreno Valley, Colton and Riverside (2075), plus about 90 name lookups.
- **Wayback:** ARMC news-blog and ruhealth.org/news listings. The 2026 ARMC Match Day post lists no neurosurgery, because RUHS sponsors it. The archived RUHS neurosurgery pages 2017-26 carry no names.
- **Common Crawl:** all 7 crawls that failed in phase 1 were retried successfully: 0 records, or a parked page (arrowheadneurosurgery.org 2017-08). The low-yield ruhealth.org path scan was stopped after CC-MAIN-2017-13.
- **ruhsneurosurgery.com:** still password-protected and not bypassed. It remains the only known roster source.
