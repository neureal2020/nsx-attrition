# Program 125: Wayne State University / Detroit Medical Center (Detroit, MI). CLOSED

7-year program (residency.php: "seven years ... one year of research"). About 1-3 interns a year. DMC was the sponsor, and the rosters sat on the WSU department site.

## Closure (phase 2)
- **Type:** loss of ACGME accreditation, not a voluntary closure. UC news (2020-06-10, written before Match Day on 2020-03-20) says Gaulden transferred "when she learned of Wayne State losing its accreditation".
- **Last academic year: 2019-20.** The residents dispersed during the year and at its end:
  - Huang went to Minnesota mid-year (by May 2020).
  - Gaulden went to Cincinnati in March 2020.
  - Everyone else had left by July 2020.
- **When it was decided:** between Aug 2019 and Feb 2020, most likely fall 2019.
  - `/education` still linked "Current Residents" on 2019-08-10.
  - `/residents` was emptied by 2019-11-17.
  - The residency paragraph was dropped from `/message` between 2019-10-31 and 2019-12-30.
- **Effective date: not found.** The end of AY 2019-20 (2020-06-30) is the most likely date. The DMC still named Neurosurgery on its GME pages until 2020-09 and 2020-10; the mentions were gone by 2020-12 and 2021-01.
- **ACGME and FREIDA:** Wayback holds no program page for this program.
- **2020-21 onward:** closed. The only open question is Garling, who would have been PGY-7. He may have finished under a teach-out at DMC.
