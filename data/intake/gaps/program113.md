# Program 113: University of Virginia Medical Center Program (Charlottesville, VA)

The residency is 7 years long, with about 3 UVA-track residents per year.

Until the 2020 class, UVA's pages also listed the joint NIH-UVA track, marked "(NIH)". Those residents belong to program 14 and are excluded here, matched by name against `rosters_program14.json`: Weintraub, Mehta, Ho, Ksendzovsky, Maggio, Mastorakos, Pomeraniec, Ampie, Asuzu, Diamond, Laws.
