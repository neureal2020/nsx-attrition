Her later affiliations are MUSC Surgery (2021) and Cape Fear Valley general/acute care/trauma surgery (2024). She left neurosurgery either after PGY-1 (June 2011) or during or after PGY-2 (2011-12); which one is unknown. She is recorded as a specialty switch in both cases. Buchholz (PGY-2, 2011) and Wright (PGY-3, 2012) both joined her 2010 class.
