                                                                          Data Analysis
                                                                                 ␹2 tests of independence and other tests were used for com-
                                                                          paring proportions. All variables were entered simultaneously
                                                                          (blockwise) into a multivariable logistic regression analysis and
                                                                          assessed for significance. Statistical significance was defined as an
                                                                          unadjusted P value less than 0.05 in a 2-tailed test. The categorical
                                                                          variables in the study were coded prior to being entered into the
                                                                          regression equation. Marital status was coded so that single was
                                                                          equal to 0 and married was equal to 1. Race was coded so that
                                                                          minority was coded as 0 and nonminority was coded as 1. The
                                                                          children variable was coded so that residents without children were
                                                                          coded as 0, whereas residents with children were coded as 1. As
                                                                          PGY-level was unknown for the research residents, they were
                                                                          excluded from the regression analysis. Data were analyzed using
                                                                          SPSS 14 (SPSS Inc., Chicago, IL).
