# PDFs to download (paywalled or blocked; only abstracts were read)

Most important first. Download through institutional access.

1. **Analysis of national trends in neurosurgical resident attrition.** Agarwal N et al. *J Neurosurg*, 2019;131(5):1668-73. https://doi.org/10.3171/2018.5.JNS18519. Needed for: the breakdown by outcome/PGY/program, and for recomputing the rate without NS-to-NS transfers.
2. **Positive trends in neurosurgery enrollment and attrition: analysis of the 2000-2009 female neurosurgery resident cohort.** Renfrow JJ et al. *J Neurosurg*, 2016;124(3):834-9. https://doi.org/10.3171/2015.3.JNS142313. Needed for: how transfers were handled, and the PGY distribution.
3. **Attrition rates in neurosurgery residency: analysis of 1361 consecutive residents matched from 1990 to 1999.** Lynch G et al. *J Neurosurg*, 2015;122(2):240-9. https://doi.org/10.3171/2014.10.JNS132436. Needed for: the completion definition and how transfers were handled.
4. **Neurosurgery Resident Attrition Rates Defy Trends and Decrease During COVID-19 Pandemic.** Kabangu JK et al. *World Neurosurg*, 2023;179:e374-9. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2023.08.093. PubMed flags it as a free article, but ScienceDirect showed a CAPTCHA. Needed for: yearly rates, PGY transfer rates, and the unverified 3.32%→1.6% trend.
5. **Evaluating Match and Attrition Rates for Women and African Americans in Neurosurgery.** Kabangu JK et al. *Neurosurgery*, 2023;92(4):695-702. https://doi.org/10.1227/neu.0000000000002257. Needed for: its public-roster method, which is closest to ours.
6. **Trends in United States neurosurgery residency education and training over the last decade (2009-2019).** Yaeger KA et al. *Neurosurg Focus*, 2020;48(3):E6. https://doi.org/10.3171/2019.12.FOCUS19827. Needed for: the year-by-year graduation/attrition table.
7. **Prevalence, management, and outcome of problem residents among neurosurgical training programs in the United States.** Raman HS et al. *J Neurosurg*, 2019;130(1):322-6. https://doi.org/10.3171/2017.8.JNS171719. Needed for: the 20-year overall program attrition figure.
8. **Gender parity in neurosurgery residencies: an analysis.** Kabangu JK et al. *J Neurosurg*, 2024;141(1):48-54. https://doi.org/10.3171/2023.11.JNS231152.
9. **Cutting Ties: General Surgery Residents Have Higher Attrition Rates Than Residents in Other Surgical Training Programs.** Sundel M et al. *J Surg Educ*, 2024;81(7):900-4. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsurg.2024.04.004. Needed for: the neurosurgery annual rate for 2012-22.
10. **An analysis of attrition from Canadian neurosurgery residency programs.** Cusimano MD et al. *Acad Med*, 1999;74(8):925-31. https://doi.org/10.1097/00001888-199908000-00019. Needed for: verifying the 42.6% total rate.
11. **Prevalence and Causes of Attrition Among Surgical Residents: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.** Khoushhal Z et al. *JAMA Surg*, 2017;152(3):265-72. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamasurg.2016.4086.
12. **Status of Resident Attrition From Surgical Residency in the Past, Present, and Future Outlook.** Shweikeh F et al. *J Surg Educ*, 2018;75(2):254-62. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsurg.2017.07.015.
13. **A national study of attrition in general surgery training: which residents leave and where do they go?** Yeo H et al. *Ann Surg*, 2010;252(3):529-34. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0b013e3181f2789c.
14. **Factors associated with burnout among US neurosurgery residents: a nationwide survey.** *J Neurosurg*, 2018;129(5):1349-63. https://thejns.org/view/journals/j-neurosurg/129/5/article-p1349.xml. Context only; needed to verify the 41% "thought of quitting" figure.
15. **Risk Factors for Dropping Out of Neurosurgical Residency Programs - A Survey Study.** Ottenhausen M et al. *World Neurosurg*, 2018;120:e100-6. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2018.07.195. Non-US; low priority.

Already open (no download needed): Haruno 2023 JAMA Surg (PMC9909577). Worth saving its Table 2/eTables to confirm the neurosurgery female RR. Also open: Yeo 2018 (PMC5875388), Adams 2017 CJS, Wang 2022, and the Far 2024 AANS abstract.
